# YouTube Kids Content Quality Standards: Agentic Specification Framework

This skill defines the operational framework for producing, auditing, and optimizing YouTube Kids content. It separates the execution into three core pillars:
1. **Destinations (Конечные цели):** What end outcomes must be achieved.
2. **Guardrails (Жесткие ограничения):** Non-negotiable safety and quality boundaries (containing the 34 strict rules).
3. **Definition of Done (Критерии качества):** Verifiable criteria required before publishing.
4. **Execution Freedom (Путь исполнения):** Complete creative freedom granted to the model/creator to generate stories, formats, and visuals within the defined constraints.

---

## 1. Destinations (Конечные цели и целевые состояния)

Every video and channel optimization project must target the following end states:

- **Destination 1: YouTube Kids App Eligibility**
  Content satisfies all automated and manual curation benchmarks for inclusion on the standalone YouTube Kids app.

- **Destination 2: High Algorithmic Recommendation & Discovery**
  Maximized distribution across YouTube home feeds, suggested sidebar recommendations, and kid-safe search loops.

- **Destination 3: 100% Monetization Safety & Premium Ad Tiering**
  Clean brand-safety rating (green monetization status) unlocking high-RPM family-friendly ad inventories.

- **Destination 4: High-Retention Educational & Prosocial Impact**
  Strong audience retention, high parent trust ratings, and active engagement driven by curiosity and joy.

---

## 2. Guardrails (Жесткие ограничения — 34 Правила)

Guardrails are hard, non-negotiable boundaries. Any breach results in immediate failure and candidate rejection.

### 2.1 Content Prohibitions & Safety Boundaries (Rules 6–19)
- **Guardrail 1 (Rule 6): Zero Minor Depiction Safety Violations** — No mistreatment, bullying, sexualization, semi-nudity, or revealing clothing on children.
- **Guardrail 2 (Rule 7): Zero Dangerous / Harmful Scenes** — No medical procedures, syringes, physical pain, violence (even in gaming), or hazardous stunts.
- **Guardrail 3 (Rule 8): Zero Horror or Repulsive Elements** — No monsters, jump scares, toilet humor, vomiting, or children shown in distress.
- **Guardrail 4 (Rule 9): Zero Negative Behavior Modeling** — No insults, physical aggression, bullying, or exposure to tobacco/alcohol/substances.
- **Guardrail 5 (Rule 10): Zero Over-Commercialization** — No aggressive brand pitching, pure promotional ads, or non-stop unboxing consumerism.
- **Guardrail 6 (Rule 11): Zero Manipulative / Deceptive Tropes** — No using popular characters/keywords in inappropriate, weird, or deceptive contexts.
- **Guardrail 7 (Rule 12): Zero Kidnapping / Abduction Themes** — No missing child or abduction storylines under any circumstances (even as pretend play).
- **Guardrail 8 (Rule 13): Zero Fear-Based Depictions** — No terrified, screaming, or hysterically crying children/characters.
- **Guardrail 9 (Rule 14): Restricted Unhealthy Food** — No promoting candy, soda, fast food, or sweets (permitted only in explicit healthy-nutrition lessons).
- **Guardrail 10 (Rule 15): Zero Over-the-Top Shock Reactions** — No hyper-dramatic reactions like open-mouth shock, extreme rage, or dramatic weeping.
- **Guardrail 11 (Rule 16): Zero Mature Tropes & Negative Characters** — No *Squid Game*, Labubu, horror-adjacent toys, or adult series adaptations.
- **Guardrail 12 (Rule 17): Zero Prison / Crime Themes** — Never depict a child as a criminal, thief, or inmate behind bars.
- **Guardrail 13 (Rule 18): Zero Scary Doctors / Medical Manipulation** — No portraying medical care or doctors in a scary or punitive light.
- **Guardrail 14 (Rule 19): Enforce Full Swimwear Coverage** — Ensure age-appropriate full-coverage attire for beach/pool settings.

### 2.2 Thumbnail Visual Boundaries (Rules 20–28)
- **Guardrail 15 (Rule 20):** No screaming or artificially exaggerated open-mouth shock faces.
- **Guardrail 16 (Rule 21):** No physical fights, aggressive confrontations, or hostile "This vs That" conflict graphics.
- **Guardrail 17 (Rule 22):** No misleading visuals or sensationalized clickbait graphics.
- **Guardrail 18 (Rule 23):** No cages, jail bars, boxes, or physical confinement imagery.
- **Guardrail 19 (Rule 24):** No scary entities (monsters, aggressive dinosaurs, ghosts, gorillas).
- **Guardrail 20 (Rule 25):** No crying, fearful, or emotionally distressed children on thumbnails.
- **Guardrail 21 (Rule 26):** No oversized, giant cutout faces that overwhelm the visual balance.
- **Guardrail 22 (Rule 27):** No hazardous activities or adult roleplay (kids driving cars, "doctor play").
- **Guardrail 23 (Rule 28):** No prominent junk food, fast food, sugary treats, or soda cans.

### 2.3 Metadata Boundaries (Rules 29–34)
- **Guardrail 24 (Rule 29):** No ALL CAPS formatting in titles or descriptions.
- **Guardrail 25 (Rule 30):** Zero keyword stuffing or tag duplication.
- **Guardrail 26 (Rule 31):** No superlative hype phrases ("Best Video Ever", "You Won't Believe This!").
- **Guardrail 27 (Rule 32):** Zero fake educational claims.
- **Guardrail 28 (Rule 33):** No overly dramatic, complex, or misleading description copy.
- **Guardrail 29 (Rule 34):** Zero discrepancy between metadata promises, thumbnail graphics, and video footage.

## 3. Definition of Done (Критерии качества)

Before any video asset or channel configuration is signed off, it must satisfy this empirical Definition of Done:

### 3.1 Positive Value Verification (Rules 1–5 Checklist)
- [ ] **Rule 1 (Prosocial Values):** Content actively models sharing, helping, kindness, or cooperation.
- [ ] **Rule 2 (Curiosity Spark):** Content stimulates natural learning about science, animals, nature, or everyday life.
- [ ] **Rule 3 (Creativity & Play):** Content inspires active building, crafting, drawing, or imaginative play.
- [ ] **Rule 4 (Resilience & Problem-Solving):** Content demonstrates constructive conflict resolution or handling challenges smoothly.
- [ ] **Rule 5 (Diversity & Inclusion):** Content represents diverse cultures, backgrounds, or perspectives respectfully.

### 3.2 Thumbnail DoD Matrix
| Metric | Verification Standard | Pass/Fail |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Emotion** | Smiles, curiosity, or natural joy (0% shock / fear / tears) | [ ] |
| **Dynamics** | Teamwork, play, or friendship (0% fighting / "This vs That") | [ ] |
| **Safety** | Clean environment (0% bars, cages, dangerous tasks, scary monsters) | [ ] |
| **Food & Commercial** | Healthy items or neutral props (0% junk food, soda, ads) | [ ] |
| **Composition** | Balanced proportions, clear subject (0% giant overpowering faces) | [ ] |

### 3.3 Metadata DoD Checklist
- [ ] Title is concise, truthful, descriptive, and formatted in sentence/title case (No ALL CAPS).
- [ ] Description provides clear, simple context for parents and kids.
- [ ] Tags are clean, focused, and non-repetitive.
- [ ] 100% alignment exists between title, thumbnail imagery, and actual video content.

## 4. Execution Freedom (Путь исполнения для Модели)

Within the **Destinations**, **Guardrails**, and **Definition of Done**, the AI model / content creator has **full autonomy** over the creative execution path:

- **Format Autonomy:** Choose freely between live-action, 2D/3D animation, claymation, puppet shows, stop-motion, or vlogs.
- **Storytelling & Plot:** Construct original narratives, mystery-solving adventures, musical songs, craft tutorials, or scientific experiments.
- **Visual Design & Aesthetics:** Select vibrant color schemes, custom character designs, musical score, lighting, and pacing optimal for the age segment.
- **Hook Strategy:** Craft intro hooks driven by surprise, humor, music, or intrigue—without relying on fear, clickbait, or loud jump scares.
